The Comeback

One wrong “Here we go” cost you everything. Someone fed you that tip on purpose. Rebuild your name, from a free blog to the front page, and find out who burned you.

The fall (prologue film)

Deadline Day, 23:58, the Chronicle newsroom. Your best contact, super-agent Vince Marlow, rings with the exclusive: the club’s star is signing tonight, pen on paper by midnight. You hit publish. HERE WE GO.

At 00:03 the player signs somewhere else. By breakfast you are a meme and 38,200 followers are gone. Editor Mags Doyle slides an empty box across the desk without a word. On the bus home, your phone lights up. Unknown number: “Nothing personal.”

Cast

You. The byline the player names. Talented, burned and underestimated. You never speak on screen; your posts speak for you.

Mags Doyle, the Chronicle editor. She fired you and she runs the inbox. She's gruff, fair, and quietly keeping score.

Vince Marlow, super-agent. Sunglasses at night. He planted the fake to push a club into overpaying. He is the man behind the text.

Hana Okafor, Evening Post editor. She hires you when nobody else will. She's the first person who believes in you.

@BackPageBants. The tabloid account run by Carl Stubbs at the Daily Roar. It made a highlight reel of your fall, and Vince feeds it.

@ITK_Kev. Anonymous and always a day early. It's secretly Priya, the Chronicle intern who was in the room that night. First a rival, then your ally.

@PressBoxPete. The old-school insider: slow, respected, never wrong on purpose. He's the bar you have to clear.

Your five sources. Dougie, the kit man at your boyhood club, is the first to call back. Sal, the barber, hears everything under the clippers. Rosa Lindqvist is an agent and Vince’s rival, with her own score to settle. Terminal Tony spots at the airport and misses nothing. Dr Inès, the physio, reads a body like a contract.

How the story is told

The story is told through short films with no dialogue. The characters act and you read what they mean: Sal sweeps up and books a haircut for next month, so the player is staying. Words live in-world: the inbox, the Feed, front pages and TV tickers. Each chapter has an opening film, one mid-chapter reveal and a promotion film. All of the story runs on the career you already play (windows, credibility, followers), so there are no separate story missions to grind.

Chapter 4 · Level 4The WarChief

@BackPageBants goes all in, and Vince starts planting fakes in your windows. The game marks one saga per window as Vince’s play, where one source’s read is poisoned, and you learn who lies from what they do. Reveal: @ITK_Kev messages you. It’s Priya: “I was there that night. I kept the call log.”

Vince’s play in every window · Goal: 56 windows, credibility 85
Films: Carl Stubbs laughs at a TV showing your old post · Priya pushes a USB stick across a café table · Mags Doyle calls you. Not the other way round.
Chapter 5 · Ultimate levelThe ChronicleTier One

Back at the same building, the same desk, the same chair, with a different ending. You need three Tier 1 windows. Finale: Deadline Day again. Vince rings with a “Here we go” on a star, word for word the same pitch. Your sources’ actions say otherwise. You call it right and run the story on Vince and the Roar.

Star players, 45-second Deadline Day · Goal: 3 Tier 1 windows at Tier One rank
Films: the lift doors open onto the old newsroom · the Deadline Day clock hits 23:58 · the presses roll your front page
EpilogueThe Front PageStory complete

Your “Here we go” is on the front page, and this time it’s right. Last shot: Vince’s phone lights up. Unknown number: “Nothing personal.” You sent it. The career carries on after the story. Each new season in the real 2026/27 calendar brings a new rival arc, and your byline keeps growing.

Films: the front page framed on Mags’s wall · the text on Vince’s phone
What changes in the game. Chapter titles and ranks line up with the byline tiers everywhere (Blogger to Tier One). There are about 16 story films: the prologue, 5 openers, 5 reveals, 4 promotions and the finale with the epilogue. The editor’s inbox lines are rewritten per chapter around the mystery. One cast of rivals across every mode: @BackPageBants, @ITK_Kev and @PressBoxPete. The sources get their names. There's a new Chapter 4 mechanic, “Vince’s play”. Rules, scoring and the Daily stay untouched.
