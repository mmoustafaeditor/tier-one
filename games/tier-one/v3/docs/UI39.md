# Tier One 3.9 "Newsroom" — the UI handoff build

The owner supplied fourteen screenshots (scratchpad `uihandoff/screenshots/*.png`, 941×1672 ≈ a 390×693 phone) and said:

> "take out the current scene animations you have I don't want or like them, it should just be an animation screen of
> the characters and animation sounds source dependent then what they say comes up and also goes on the screen of the
> player so I can review what everyone said. Review the screenshots I have provided. Make sure you optimize the UI for
> 3 versions: web game, desktop version, and Android apk. The new UI will look like the screenshots I just presented."

The screenshots are **visual direction**, not rules: scoring, sources, numbers, follower counts and football facts come
from the engine and the save, never from the pictures. Rebuild every control as real, accessible UI (no raster text).
The films are already gone from the base (`lib/scenes.ts` is a no-op shim; `src/film`, `ui/film.tsx`, `CallScene`,
`PostScene` are deleted). This document is the contract between the three lanes. Read it whole before writing a line.

## 0. Standing owner rules (never re-ask)

- Pages do not scroll (one viewport at 390×664 and up: `fit`/`fit__body`, `usePaged`+`Pager` for lists). Where a list
  must be long (comments, contacts, market rows), the **list pane** scrolls inside the page; the page itself does not.
- **Back is a real button, top-left, on every non-home page** (`TopBar back=`), plus Esc on desktop and the Android
  back key (`window.__tierBack`).
- Names say what they are: Daily, Career, Press Rooms, Market, My Press Card, Missions, Shop, Settings. Never "HERE WE
  GO" (a real journalist's trademark). Never Hint/Post/Drop for stakes: **Talks · Advanced · Confirmed**.
- Nothing paid changes a ranked score. Don't touch The Gaffer, Saif's Android feed (`api/tier-one/latest.js`) or
  `tier-one/apk`. No API keys. No real-player likeness art (the owner parked it): real players keep the `<Portrait
  kind="player">` placeholder (initials/silhouette tinted by club).
- The owner tests on the live site; no screenshot sessions or sims unless asked. Save credits: do the work, don't
  narrate it.
- Every commit ends with the two trailer lines the session uses (Co-Authored-By · Claude-Session).

## 4. The Call screen (lane D) — the owner's one functional ask

`ui/CallScreen.tsx` replaces the film. A full-screen page (portal over the window, `role="dialog"`), one viewport:

1. **Top**: the bar with Back ("‹ On the line") and "Day 3 · Saga 01"; a serif headline per source with the coral full
   stop: kitman "A word from inside the club." · barber "Chair talk." · agent "From the player's camp." · spotter "Eyes
   on the terminal." · physio "Inside the treatment room." · leak "Off the press office." (EN/AR/ES).
2. **The character**: the source's portrait, big (the owner's WebP, `<Portrait kind="source" id size={min(60vw,
   320px)}>`), on the dark desk with a warm radial glow. **Animation of the character**: it breathes (a slow 3 s
   scale/translate loop) and, when the line lands, cross-fades to the reaction mood (`moodFor(src, o)`, 220 ms) with a
   small nod. Under reduced motion / low-data: still portrait, instant swap.
3. **Source-dependent sound** as the screen opens (synth, `lib/sfx.ts`): kitman `scene.kitman`, barber `scene.barber`
   (clippers/snips), agent `scene.agent` (the ring + babble), spotter `scene.spotter` (airport), physio `scene.physio`
   (monitor), leak `scene.leak` (fax). Then a short `voice(src)` murmur as the words come up. A short haptic.
4. **The caller row** (desk card): coral phone disc, "The Kit Man", "Club insider · 1 contact point".
5. **The quote card** (paper): a big coral “ , the line in serif (typed in over ≤ 700 ms, instant under reduced motion:
   `voiceLine(t.lang, c, clue)`), "About Leo Costa · Day 3".
6. **What it means** (desk rows): the info line ("He knows whether the player is leaving. He cannot confirm the
   destination." = `src.<k>P` / the rule line) and the chips "Club circle" + "✓ Evidence added to your file".
7. **Back to the story →** (coral). Esc / Back do the same. Nothing waits: the engine already recorded the answer; the
   quote is in the file's Clippings ("What they said") the moment the screen closes, with the "New" tag.

The same screen serves Daily, Career, Practice and Rooms (Career uses `srcNamed`).
