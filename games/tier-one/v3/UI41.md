# Tier One 4.1 — the compact rebuild (overrides CONCEPT4 §2, §6, §12, §17 wherever they disagree)

The owner's verdict on 4.0: too many features, out of place, scrolling pages, made-up app names (Blurt, Lens, DMs), a DM page for
sources, a tiny back arrow at the bottom, widgets. 3.x played better. 4.1 is a compact, menu-based game on a phone: less is more.
The rules (RULES4.md, engine4) and the economy numbers stay; the screens are rebuilt.

## The five laws (every screen, no exceptions)

1. **No page scrolls.** Every screen fits the viewport (390×664 up to desktop) inside `ui/screen.tsx` `<Screen>`. Lists page with
   `<Pager>` (fixed rows + ‹ 2/5 ›). If it doesn't fit, cut content or split into tabs; never add a scrollbar.
2. **Back is a real button, top-left, on every page** (`<Screen onBack>`), plus Esc. The bottom home-bar arrow is removed.
3. **Names say what they are.** Daily Challenge, Career, Deadline Day, Transfer Wire, Rooms, Press Card, Missions, Shop, Settings.
   Never Blurt, Lens, DMs, Market, Groups, Boards, Story-as-app-name.
4. **One screen, one job, one main button.** The main action sits in the `<Screen footer>` and is the biggest thing on the page.
5. **Formatted like a shipped game.** Buttons fit their labels (no clipping, no wrapping mid-word), content centred on its grid,
   text contrast ≥ 4.5:1 on every surface and wallpaper, tap targets ≥ 44px, one type scale, one spacing scale (4/8/12/16/24),
   EN/AR (RTL)/ES all fit. Desktop: the phone is centred on the desk at a fixed phone size; nothing stretches.

## The phone

- **Lock screen** (title screen): time, date, your Press Card stamp (handle · rank), "Tap to open". One tap opens the home screen.
  Lock faces stay as a cosmetic (Shop).
- **Home screen**: a clean grid of big labelled app tiles, nothing else (NO widgets — delete `ui/widgets.tsx`, `lib/widgets.ts` and
  every widget reference). A slim header with your handle, level and coins. Red badge dots (iOS-style, top-right of a tile, with a
  count) for things that need you: Daily not played, missions done to claim, a resolved Wire call, a room round open.

| Tile | Opens |
|---|---|
| **Daily Challenge** | today's board (or its result if played) |
| **Career** | Continue / New career |
| **Deadline Day** | the Deadline Day mode (Level 3) |
| **Transfer Wire** | real-life transfers (watch free; calls at Level 2) |
| **Rooms** | play friends (Level 4) |
| **Press Card** | your stats card for every mode + leaderboards |
| **Missions** | today's and this week's missions, claim rewards |
| **Shop** | looks, phones, coins, Gold |
| **Settings** | language, sound, how to play, account |

Sponsors stay, but they live inside Press Card (a "Sponsor" tab) and offers arrive as a notification; no separate app.

## Daily Challenge (the core; like 3.x, but better)

One screen, no scroll:
- Top: "Day 2 of 5" + **3 calls left** (big), the day bar, End Day button.
- The **five players** as five compact rows/cards (name, from-club → linked club in club colours, a status chip: "no info" /
  "2 tips" / your call stamped). Tap a player → the player screen.
- **Player screen** (one screen): the player header; the **five sources as five buttons**, each showing name, what they tell you,
  how often they're right, and **its cost in calls** (barber FREE, others 1 call; locked ones say "from Day 3"). Tapping a source
  shows the call screen (name, the ambient sound, a few seconds, skip) and the answer lands on that source's button. Rival posts
  for this player show as one line each. At the bottom two buttons: **Decide now** and **Decide later**.
- **Decide sheet**: SIGNS / ELSEWHERE / STAYS (clubs named) then Hint ×1 / Post ×2 / Drop ×3, with the one-line deal ("Win 46 if he
  signs. Lose 60 if he doesn't."), **Post** button, the post animation, back to the board.
- End of window → **Results** on one screen: the five players with your call, what happened, points; total, tier stamp, XP/coins/
  followers; Share; Back to home. (A "details" tab may page through players; no scroll.)

## Deadline Day (make it beautiful)

The same board, but it's the night: a full-width **countdown clock** (90 s) as the hero, a live ticker of rival posts sliding in,
six players in a compact grid, urgency that builds (the clock turns red under 15 s, sounds tick), every source open, Post in two
taps. The results land as a "window shut" moment. Level 3; on real deadline days it's ranked (DD Live).

## Career

- Opening Career shows **Continue** (chapter, week, goal) and **New career** (confirm, wipes the old). Nothing else.
- Each chapter has one **chapter card** that explains the story in three short lines (where you are, who's against you, what you
  need to do), the boss card (handle, accuracy, your record), the goal with a progress bar, and **Play next window**. Story beats
  arrive as short messages on that card, not long text.
- Windows play on the Daily Challenge screen (career rules).

## Transfer Wire (real life)

- Filter chips: **Stars** (player rating) · **Heat** (traction) · **League** · **Team**, plus a search box. Results page with
  `<Pager>` (5 a page). Each rumour row: player, clubs, market %, heat, your call if any.
- Rumour screen: the story, market %, **Watch** (free) and **Call it** (HE MOVES / HE STAYS + Hint/Post/Drop, deal in one line).
- Tabs: Rumours · My calls (open / resolved, points). Right calls pay points, XP, coins and Tips.

## Press Card

One card per mode, tabbed: Overall (handle, rank, level, rep, followers, streak) · Daily · Career · Deadline Day · Wire · Rooms;
then Leaderboards (today / week) and Sponsor. Achievements live here (sealed ones shown sealed).

## Missions

A short list (3 daily + 3 weekly), each with progress and **Claim**. The tile badge shows how many are ready to claim.

## Cut in 4.1

Widgets, the separate DMs/contacts page, Blurt/Lens/Market/Groups/Boards names, the bottom home-bar back arrow, the desktop side
panel, edit-home mode, icon packs (keep themes, lock faces, phone skins in Shop), anything that scrolls.
