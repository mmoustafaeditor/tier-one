Tier One

Daily Challenge live

Break the deal first, or become the meme. You're a football transfer journalist with six sources, five sagas and seven days. Some sources lie. Deadline Day is 60 real seconds. Tier 1 is earned.

🕴️

Sources that lie to you

The physio, the airport spotter, the agent, the kitman, the barber, the rival leak. Each has a reliability level and sticks to its story. Ask around before you go loud.

🚨

Plot twists

One to three real deals turn mid-window. Earlier reads go stale and a right call after a twist scores extra. The week is never over on Day 3.

⏱️

Deadline Day: 60 real seconds

The app turns red, a real clock starts, and you have three contacts to fix every call you're not sure about.

👑

Tier 1 is earned

Five tiers plus the ITK Clown. Tier 1 takes 370 points; strong play gets there in about 20% of Daily windows, so a Tier 1 screenshot means something.

  • 🧰 Career mode + source upgrades
  • 🤝 Private rooms with friends
  • 🌍 English · Español · مصري

THEGAFFER

The Gaffer

New · v0.12

Build a coaching career across Europe's big five leagues and the Arab leagues. Hand the dull jobs to your staff or take control of everything, survive the summer and winter windows, and leave a trophy cabinet behind you.

🧑‍💼

Staff room

13 duties you can hand to your staff or take back at any time. Everything except playing the match. Your staff log what they did.

📺

Matchday like a broadcast

Player ratings, man of the match, xG and commentary, then a full-time card with the table move, the board, the fans and any records.

📝

Two transfer windows

Summer and winter windows with a deadline day, a shortlist, scout estimates for players outside your league, and season loans in and out.

🏆

History and records

Past seasons, club records, a trophy cabinet and the academy graduates you brought through.

  • 🏟️ Big five leagues + Arab leagues
  • 🖥️ Two-column layout on desktop
  • 🌍 English · العربية · Español · Français

About

Semba Studios

Semba Studios is a small indie studio making quick-to-learn, hard-to-put-down football games.

Our games are free to play in the browser, with an Android app when you want one on your home screen. Every game ships in English, Spanish and Arabic; The Gaffer adds French.

We're a tiny human team working side by side with AI, and every release is logged in Updates above. Ideas, bugs, banter: write to us.
