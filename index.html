Why it hooks

Everyone's an ITK until Deadline Day.

Built for people who refresh the transfer feed at 11pm. Every mechanic is there to make you sweat before you post.

🕴️

Sources that lie to you

The physio, the airport spotter, the agent, the kitman, the barber, the rival newsroom leak. Each has a reliability level, each sticks to its story, and the agent is usually talking the deal up. Ask around before you go loud.

🚨

Plot twists

Exactly two real deals turn mid-window. Earlier reads go stale, that player's sources open up again, and a right call after a twist scores extra. The week is never over on Day 3.

⏱️

Deadline Day: 60 real seconds

The app turns red, a real-time clock starts, and you have three contacts to fix every call you're not sure about. It's the best minute in football and now it's a game.
